The 30,000 target clears in month 16, not month 12. An earlier version of this forecast had it at month 12. That version credited a valuation enquiry as revenue in the month it arrived — but a valuation enquiry does not become a signed listing for two to four months. Applying a three-month lag pushes the first AED 30,000 month to 16. The steady state is AED 31,788–87,548 a month.

Concentration risk, stated plainly. Roughly 92% of the low-case revenue is one valuation-to-listing conversion per month. If that single conversion does not happen, the month is worth about AED 2,000 in referral fees. Snagging and holiday-home traffic is real and cheap to win, but it does not pay a commission by itself — it exists to feed the valuation page. Any month where the valuation page is not converting, the site is a well-ranked blog.

05 — Build Order

What the council settled

Wave 1 — the till and the door

Valuation pages plus the snagging cluster. The valuation page must exist before any traffic arrives, because it is the only page that produces a commission. Snagging is built alongside it because it is the cheapest traffic on the board.

Wave 2 — the second door

Holiday-home and handover pages. Both feed owners into the valuation page. The short-let versus long-let comparison is the strongest cross-sell on the site: it ends with an owner deciding what to do with an asset.

Wave 3 — depth

Mortgage and permit pages, as blog-tier. Mortgage incumbents sit at 198 referring domains, so these will not rank quickly and should not hold up the launch.

The one rule that makes the funnel work. Every page in waves 2 and 3 carries one line linking to the valuation page, phrased as a question the reader is already asking — "wondering whether to sell instead of re-letting?" Without that line, snagging and holiday-home traffic is impressions with no commercial destination.

06 — The Hidden-Gem Hunt

What a deeper search actually found

A dedicated hunt was run for low-competition, high-value long-tail that could rank in weeks rather than months. It returned two findings, both negative, and both worth more than a padded keyword list would have been.

Finding 1 — the community long-tail does not exist

401 combinations were priced: every service crossed with 27 Dubai communities, plus 20 question-shaped queries. 98% returned zero measurable volume. "snagging inspection jvc", "airbnb management dubai marina", "how much does snagging cost in dubai" — none of it registers.

Zero here means under roughly ten searches a month, not literally nobody. Those terms can still be ranked for as sections inside a page. They cannot carry a revenue forecast.

Finding 2 — the one gem that looked real was a mirage

Off-plan buying terms looked outstanding: 320 searches/mo at AED 41.35 a click with an ad-competition index of 0.10 — and off-plan is 70% of Dubai deals and pays commission, not a referral fee.

Then the organic barrier came back: the off-plan microsites holding page one carry a median of 747 referring domains (offplan-dubai.com 747, opr.ae 1,352, dxboffplan.com 1,367). Not takeable by a new domain.

The lesson worth keeping: ad competition is not organic difficulty. The two diverge sharply in this market, and screening on the competition index alone would have moved the entire plan onto unwinnable ground.

07 — The Month 2–3 Question

Answered directly: organic cannot do it

The AED 3,000 by month 2–3 target is not achievable from organic search. The forecast gives AED 66 in month 2 and AED 133 in month 3. No keyword selection changes that — the gem hunt above was the attempt, and it found nothing rankable in eight weeks that also carries volume. A new domain does not rank in sixty days, and the commission that makes this business work lags a further three months behind the enquiry.

There is exactly one honest route to revenue in that window, and it uses the same search results — bought rather than earned while the pages mature.

The paid bridge, at four conversion assumptions

The click price is measured. The conversion rates are not — they are assumptions, and a brand-new site with no reviews and no track record converts at the low end of this table, not the high end.

The bridge returns more than it costs at every point on that table, including the worst realistic case (1% enquiry rate, one listing per four enquiries: AED 18,540 per listing, 1.4x–3.8x). What changes is the budget required for one listing a month — AED 4,700 if conversion is good, AED 18,500 if it is poor. Budget for the pessimistic number and be pleased to spend less.

Two risks that could stall this in month 1. First, Google requires advertiser identity verification for property advertising in the UAE; a newly-licensed broker can lose two to three weeks to that process, which is precisely when the bridge is needed. Start verification before writing a single page. Second, the one-in-three close rate assumes a valuation enquiry is called back fast. Set a hard rule of fifteen minutes — without it, that ratio is fiction and the whole model falls over.

08 — Revised Build Order

Cut to what one person can actually run

The thirteen-page launch was wrong for a solo operator. One person cannot build thirteen pages, run and tune a paid campaign, and call back valuation leads within fifteen minutes. Pages get built, leads go cold, and the conversion assumption that carries the entire forecast quietly breaks. The scope below is what survives contact with a single pair of hands.

Weeks 1–2 · One page and the ads

Build only /property-valuation-dubai/ — the page the ads point at. Start Google advertiser verification on day one. Nothing else is touched. This is the only page with a revenue path this quarter.

Weeks 3–8 · Prove the conversion rate

Run the campaign narrow. Measure the real click-to-enquiry rate against the table above and re-budget from the measured number, not the assumed one. Answer every enquiry in fifteen minutes.

Months 3–6 · Then the organic pages

Snagging first (lowest barrier at 79 referring domains, highest click price), then holiday-home. These are referral-fee work worth AED 150–2,000 — they do not touch the month 2–3 target and must not delay the page that does.

What this changes about the forecast. Nothing in the organic model moves — month 16 is still when organic alone clears AED 30,000. What changes is that revenue starts in month 1 or 2 from paid rather than month 9 from organic, and the organic pages become the thing that lets the ad budget fall away later instead of the only thing carrying the plan.

# Internal linking

Descriptive internal links are a confirmed ranking factor and the one authority lever entirely
under your control. They are free. Use them deliberately.

## Rules

1. **Anchor text describes the destination.** "snagging costs in Dubai", never "click here".
2. **Every page links up to its pillar** and across to at least one sibling.
3. **Every page links once — exactly once — to `/property-valuation-dubai/`**, late in the body,
   phrased as a question the reader is already asking. Not a banner. Not a sidebar. One sentence.
4. **No orphan pages.** Every page must be reachable from at least one other page.
5. **Navigation and breadcrumbs must be server-rendered.** A JavaScript-only menu hides the entire
   link graph from crawlers.

## The cross-link sentence

Write it fresh per page. It must read as the reader's own next question:

- On `/snagging-dubai/cost/` — "If the snag list turns out worse than expected and you are
  reconsidering the purchase, a current valuation is the first number you need."
- On `/short-let-vs-long-let/` — "If the numbers push you toward selling rather than letting,
  start with what the unit is actually worth today."

**Never** "Contact us for a free valuation!" — that is an advert, and it converts worse.

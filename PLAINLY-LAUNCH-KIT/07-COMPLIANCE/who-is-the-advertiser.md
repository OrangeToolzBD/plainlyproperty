# Who is the advertiser — read this before anything else

**Plainly holds no licence.** No RERA registration, no BRN, no ORN, no UAE trade licence.
Everything in this kit is built on that fact. If it changes, revisit this file first.

## What that rules out, absolutely

| Not allowed | Why |
|---|---|
| Any licence number presented as Plainly's | Fabricating regulatory credentials. Worst failure available here. |
| "RERA-licensed", "licensed brokerage", "our BRN" | Same. Plainly is not licensed. |
| Introducing a buyer or seller for a fee | **That is brokerage.** Unlicensed brokerage is an offence in Dubai. |
| Advertising any property | Requires a Trakheesi permit held by a licensed advertiser. |
| Quoting a valuation as Plainly's own professional opinion | A valuation for a transaction is a licensed activity. |

**A blank licence field is worse than no field.** The previous version of this kit carried
`[BRN NUMBER]` and `[ORN NUMBER]` placeholders throughout. They have been deleted, not emptied.
If you find one anywhere, delete the whole field — do not fill it.

## What Plainly actually is

An **independent information resource**. It publishes what things cost, what the rules are and
what the numbers mean. It earns by referring readers to businesses that *are* licensed to act.

## How the valuation page must work

The valuation page is the one that touches regulated territory. It works like this:

1. Plainly explains how valuation works, what it costs and what the methods are — **information**.
2. The reader requests a valuation.
3. The request is passed to **one named RERA-licensed partner brokerage**.
4. **That partner's ORN is displayed, attributed to them**, alongside a line stating plainly that
   Plainly is an information service and the partner is the licensed party.
5. The partner performs the valuation and any subsequent listing. **Plainly is paid a lead fee.**

### The disclosure line — use it verbatim

> Plainly is an information service and is not a licensed property brokerage. Valuations and
> sales are carried out by [PARTNER NAME], ORN [PARTNER ORN], registered with the Dubai Land
> Department.

That sentence appears on the valuation page and anywhere a partner is named. It is not
small print — it belongs in the body, where a reader sees it.

## Before the valuation page goes live

- [ ] A named licensed partner is contracted, in writing
- [ ] Their ORN is verified against the DLD register
- [ ] The lead-fee arrangement is documented and does not describe Plainly as introducing
      buyers or sellers for a commission
- [ ] **A UAE lawyer has confirmed the arrangement does not itself constitute brokerage.**
      This is the one item on which the whole model rests, and it is not a question this kit
      can answer.

## The commercial consequence, stated plainly

As a lead vendor rather than the broker, the site earns roughly **a third** of the original
model: **AED 10,132–29,243 per month** at steady state, against AED 31,788–87,548 had it held the
licence. That is the price of not being licensed, and it is worth knowing before the build
rather than after.

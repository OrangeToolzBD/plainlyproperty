# On-page standard — written to be quoted

Answer engines retrieve *passages*, then synthesise and cite. The job is to be easy to extract,
specific, and verifiable.

## The six rules

1. **Answer-first opening.** The first **40–60 words** of every page completely answer its core
   question and stand alone when quoted out of context. Then expand — inverted pyramid.

2. **Question-matched headings.** H2s are the literal questions people type. "How much does
   snagging cost in Dubai?" not "Pricing". Mine them from the research pack's `gaps_in_top5`
   field, from autocomplete, and from People Also Ask.

3. **Fact density — the strongest single lever.** One concrete, sourced statistic every
   **150–200 words**. Peer-reviewed testing (KDD-2024, Princeton-led) measured roughly **+41%**
   AI visibility from this alone, and **~+115%** for pages starting around position 5 — which is
   exactly where a new site sits. At 900–1,300 words that is 6–8 sourced figures per page.

4. **Self-contained chunks.** Re-state the subject rather than using "it" or "this". Front-load
   the key fact in each paragraph. Short paragraphs. Lists for steps, tables for comparisons,
   a definition sentence for anything "what is".

5. **Comparison tables.** These get reproduced near-verbatim inside AI answers. Every page that
   can carry one, should.

6. **Freshness.** A visible last-reviewed date on every page, and it must be true.

## What NOT to do — these are measured non-factors

| Myth | Reality |
|---|---|
| Keyword density / TF-IDF / "LSI keywords" | No target density exists. LSI is not a real thing. |
| Repeating keywords to win AI citations | **Disproven** in testing — it did not raise citation rates. |
| Exact-match domain | No boost; over-optimisation is a demotion risk. |
| Meta keywords tag | Ignored since ~2009. |
| Bounce rate from your analytics | Not a ranking input. |
| Buying ads for organic lift | No effect on organic whatsoever. |

Getting one of these wrong in front of a client destroys credibility faster than any missed
opportunity. Do not ship folklore.

## E-E-A-T — Trust is the load-bearing letter

This is YMYL content: it concerns people's money. That means the highest quality bar.

- Real named author on every page. Do not imply brokerage credentials the site does not hold.
- No licence number claimed for Plainly. Partner ORN attributed to the partner.
- Every figure attributed to a named authority with a date.
- Outbound citations to DLD, RERA, DET, GDRFA and the UAE statute books. Linking out to credible
  sources reads as trustworthy to both Google and answer engines — the "link equity leak" fear is
  folklore.

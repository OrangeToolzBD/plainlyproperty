# Measurement

## What to track, and how often

| Signal | Where | Cadence | What good looks like |
|---|---|---|---|
| Indexation ratio | Search Console | weekly | >80% of submitted URLs indexed by week 6 |
| Impressions by page | Search Console | weekly | rising from week 4 |
| Average position by cluster | Search Console | monthly | snagging inside top 20 by month 4 |
| Click-through rate | Search Console | monthly | above 3% once on page one — if not, rewrite the title |
| Click → enquiry | GA4 + form | weekly | this is the number the whole budget depends on |
| Enquiry → listing | CRM or a spreadsheet | monthly | one in three is the assumption; measure the truth |
| AI citation rate | manual, see below | monthly | any citation at all in months 1–6 is ahead of plan |
| Referring domains | any backlink tool | monthly | 30–60 by month 6 clears a third of the incumbents |

## The AI citation check — do it properly

Answers vary run to run; a single check tells you nothing. Take the eight questions this site
answers. Run each **five times** on **each** of ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Claude, separately.
Record a rate — "cited 3 of 5 runs on Perplexity" — never a yes/no.

Log a dated baseline **before launch**, so there is something to compare against later.

## The two numbers that decide everything

1. **Click → enquiry rate.** Every budget figure in this kit scales off it. Measure it by week 6
   and re-plan from the measured number, not the assumed one.
2. **Speed to callback.** If enquiries are not called within 15 minutes, the close rate drops and
   no amount of traffic fixes it.

## What not to react to

A single week's ranking movement is noise. A single AI answer that omits you is noise. Act on a
sustained shift across a month, not on a reading. Log everything, react quarterly.

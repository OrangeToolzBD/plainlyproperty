# 90-day runbook

Written for one person with no team. Scope is deliberately narrow — the failure mode here is
building thirteen pages while enquiries go cold.

## Days 1–14

Advertiser verification started on day one. Technical gate passed. Page 01 built and live.
Campaign running on valuation terms. Every enquiry called within 15 minutes.

**Nothing else is touched.** Not the snagging pages, not the blog, not the design polish.

## Days 15–45

Measure the click-to-enquiry rate weekly and re-budget from it. Build the snagging cluster —
pages 04, 05, 06 — using the content pipeline, one batch. Snagging has the lowest competitor
barrier on the board and the highest click prices; it is the cheapest traffic available.

Start link outreach: one industry directory, one supplier or partner page, one local publication.
Three referring domains in this window is a good month.

## Days 46–90

Build the holiday-home cluster (pages 09, 10, 11) and `/handover-dubai/`. Publish the first
month's AI-citation check against the pre-launch baseline.

Review: is the measured click-to-enquiry rate above or below 1.5%? Above, and the paid budget can
scale. Below, and the landing page needs work before more traffic is bought — more clicks at a bad
conversion rate just spends money faster.

## What success looks like at day 90

| | Realistic | Ahead of plan |
|---|---|---|
| Pages live | 7 | 10 |
| Indexation | >80% | >90% |
| Organic impressions/mo | 3,000–5,000 | 7,000+ |
| Organic clicks/mo | 40–90 | 150+ |
| Referring domains | 10–20 | 30+ |
| Listings won (paid + organic) | 1–3 | 4+ |

Organic revenue in this window is **near zero** — the forecast shows AED 133 in month 3 and that
is honest. Revenue in the first quarter comes from the paid bridge. The organic pages are what let
the ad budget fall away from month 9 onward, not what pays the bills in month 2.

## The one thing that must not slip

**Every enquiry called within 15 minutes.** Everything else in this kit is recoverable. That is not.

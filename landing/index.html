MethodWhat it usesWhen it appliesTypical cost

A broker’s valuation and a certified valuation are different documents with different legal weight. Only the third is issued through the Land Department, and only the third is the one an authority will act on.

What the holiday home management fee covers, at each end of the range
FeeUsually includedUsually charged on top
15% of gross Listing, pricing, guest communication, check-in Linen, cleaning per turnover, guest consumables
25% of gross All of the above, plus linen, cleaning and consumables Maintenance call-outs, the DET permit, tourism dirham

The percentage on its own does not tell you which is cheaper to run. A 15% fee that excludes cleaning, linen and consumables can cost more per occupied night than a 25% fee that includes them, and the gap widens with turnover. Short-let grosses more and nets less; the net depends on occupancy, the permit, tourism dirham and fees.

Questions owners actually ask

How much does snagging cost in Dubai?

AED 666–999 for a typical apartment. The price is driven by floor area and the number of systems to test, not by how many defects are found, so a badly built unit does not cost more to survey than a good one.

What is the golden visa property threshold?

A DLD-certified valuation of AED 2 million or more. An estate agent’s appraisal will not be accepted, and neither will the price you paid. The threshold applies to the certified valuation figure.

Do I need a permit to short-let in Dubai?

Yes — a holiday home permit from the Department of Economy and Tourism, issued per unit. You cannot legally short-let without one.

What do holiday home operators charge?

15–25% of gross booking revenue. The spread reflects what is bundled: a 15% fee usually excludes linen, cleaning and guest supplies, while a 25% fee usually includes them.

How long is the developer defect liability?

One year for general defects and ten years for structural failure, running from handover. What the snagging report finds inside that window is fixed at the developer’s cost.

Is re-inspection included in the snagging fee?

Usually not. Most Dubai firms price the first inspection and charge the return visit separately, and the return visit is where you confirm the developer actually fixed what was listed. Budget for both from the start.
